# Lucy N. Engine — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 2026**
**Ernest Tech House — Nairobi, Kenya**

---

## Overview

Lucy N. is a native desktop application engine. This policy explains what personal data Lucy N. collects when you use Google Sign-In, how it is stored, and what it is used for.

Short version: your data never leaves your machine. Ernest Tech House has no server receiving or storing your personal information.

---

## What We Collect

When you sign in with Google, Lucy N. receives the following from Google:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Email address | To identify you as a verified human user |
| Display name | For display purposes inside the application |
| Profile picture | For display purposes inside the application |
| Google user ID (`sub`) | To uniquely identify your session |
| Access token | To authenticate requests during your session |
| Refresh token | To silently renew your session without re-login |

We collect nothing else. We do not collect passwords. We do not collect browsing activity. We do not collect any data from websites the engine visits.

---

## Where Your Data Is Stored

**Entirely on your own machine.**

All tokens and profile information are stored locally in the Lucy N. application data directory on your device. They are encrypted at rest. They are never transmitted to Ernest Tech House servers because Ernest Tech House operates no servers that receive user data.

```
Your machine only. No cloud. No database. No third party.
```

---

## How Your Data Is Used

Your Google identity is used for one purpose:

**To establish that you are a human user**, which improves the engine's ability to make authenticated requests to third-party websites that require human verification.

Your email, name, and picture may be displayed inside Lucy N.'s own UI. Nothing else.

---

## What We Do Not Do

- We do not sell your data
- We do not share your data with any third party
- We do not send your data to Ernest Tech House servers
- We do not use your data for advertising
- We do not attach your Google identity to web scraping requests made by the engine
- We do not store your data beyond your active session unless you choose to stay signed in

---

## Google's Role

Google Sign-In is provided by Google LLC. When you sign in, you are subject to Google's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service in addition to this policy.

Google may revoke your access token at any time at their discretion.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

---

## How Long We Keep Your Data

| Scenario | What happens to your data |
|---|---|
| You sign out | Tokens deleted immediately from your machine |
| You revoke access in Google Account settings | Tokens become invalid, deleted on next engine start |
| You uninstall Lucy N. | All local data removed with the application |

---

## Your Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and applicable international law you have the right to:

- Know what data is held about you
- Request deletion of your data — sign out of Lucy N. to delete all stored tokens immediately
- Withdraw consent at any time — revoke Lucy N.'s access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

Since all data is stored locally on your machine, you are always in full control of it.

---

## Children

Lucy N. is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
